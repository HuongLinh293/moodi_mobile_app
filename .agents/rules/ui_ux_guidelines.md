# Mindra UI/UX Design Guidelines

Here are the foundational UI/UX criteria that MUST be strictly adhered to when designing and developing the Mindra application:

- **Consistency**: Maintain absolute consistency across colors, fonts, icons, border radii (bo góc), and spacing.
- **Breathable Layout**: Prioritize an open, spacious layout with fewer components, ensuring a clear focus on the core content.
- **Clear Feedback**: Every user interaction (e.g., check-ins, saving a journal entry, completing an exercise) MUST provide clear and immediate feedback (visual, haptic, or toast).
- **5-Tab Navigation**: Always maintain the 5-tab bottom navigation structure:
  1. Hôm nay (Today)
  2. Lịch & Nhật ký (Calendar & Journal)
  3. Khu vườn (Garden)
  4. Thực hành (Practice)
  5. Bạn (You)
- **Minimum Touch Target**: Ensure a minimum touch area of **44x44pt** for all interactive elements, paying special attention to emotion icons and navigation buttons.
- **User Context & Vibe**: Design for the user's specific context—ensure the app feels like a fast, private, low-pressure, and easy-to-use daily check-in tool.
- **Accessibility & Clarity**: Ensure text is highly readable, maintain good contrast, and make interactive states (normal, pressed, disabled) explicitly clear for both new and returning daily users.
